## ❓ Frequently Asked Questions

### Is wifit3 safe to use?

Yes. wifit3 is a passive scanner—it only *listens* to Wi-Fi signals. It doesn't connect to networks, change your settings, or send any data over the internet. It's like a radio tuner that shows you what stations are broadcasting nearby.

### Do I need to install anything else?

No. wifit3 is a standalone application. Once you download and extract it, you can run it immediately. No extra software or "runtimes" are required.

### Will it work on my old laptop?

Very likely. Because wifit3 is lightweight and doesn't require special permissions, it runs well even on older or lower-spec machines. If your computer can connect to Wi-Fi normally, it can run wifit3.

### Can I scan for networks in my office or school?

wifit3 works anywhere Wi-Fi is available. Just run it and it will show you the networks visible from your current location. It's perfect for checking signal strength in different rooms or verifying your own network's security.

### Is this free?

Yes, wifit3 is completely free and open-source. There are no hidden costs, trials, or subscriptions.

## 📚 Advanced Tips (For the Curious)

If you're interested in getting a bit more out of wifit3, here are a few things you can try:

- **Compare channels** – See if your Wi-Fi is on a crowded channel and consider switching to a less busy one in your router's settings.
- **Check security** – Ensure your home network is using WPA2 or WPA3. If you see WEP or "Open" on your own router, change it immediately.
- **Test signal strength** – Walk around your house while running wifit3 to find dead zones or weak spots.

These are just suggestions—wifit3 simply provides the information. The choice of what to do with it is yours.
